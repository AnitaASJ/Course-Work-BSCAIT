Why Pay Taxes

Your Contribution Builds Our Nation

Taxes are more than just money paid to the government. They are an important source of revenue used to support public services, improve infrastructure and promote Uganda's development.
When individuals and businesses meet their tax obligations, they contribute towards creating better opportunities and improving the quality of life for everyone.

Better Healthcare

Tax revenue helps support public hospitals, healthcare facilities, medical equipment and essential health services.

Quality Education

Taxes contribute to funding public schools, educational programmes, learning materials and better learning environments.

TIN Registration

A Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) is a unique identification number issued by the Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) to identify taxpayers. It helps individuals and businesses fulfil their tax obligations.

Registering for a TIN is an important step towards becoming a responsible taxpayer. Whether you are starting a business or entering employment, having a TIN helps you meet your tax responsibilities and access relevant URA services.

Steps to Register for a TIN

  1. Visit the URA Website

    Go to the official Uganda Revenue Authority website and locate the TIN registration service.

  2. Choose Your Registration Type

    Select whether you are registering as an individual or a business.

  3. Provide Your Information

    Enter the required personal or business details accurately.

  4. Review and Submit

    Check your information carefully before submitting your application.

  5. Follow the Registration Instructions

    Follow URA's instructions to complete the registration process and obtain your TIN.

Key Tax Deadlines

Stay informed about important tax filing deadlines. Filing and paying on time helps you avoid penalties and remain tax compliant.

Tax Type Filing Frequency Deadline
Value Added Tax (VAT) Monthly 15th day of the following month
Pay As You Earn (PAYE) Monthly 15th day of the following month
Withholding Tax (WHT) Monthly 15th day of the following month
Final Income Tax Annually Within 6 months after the end of the year of income

Note: Deadlines may vary depending on your tax obligations and accounting period. Always confirm the applicable dates with URA.
